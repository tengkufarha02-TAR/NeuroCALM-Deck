    IMMEDIATE GROUNDING

    5-4-3-2-1 Sensory Shock Reset

    You are safe in this physical room right now. Let your eyes land on concrete physical objects. Speak each item out loud:

    5 THINGS YOU CAN SEE

    Find five distinct colors, textures, or shapes in front of you. Name them clearly.

    4 THINGS YOU CAN TOUCH

    Feel the weight of your feet on the floor, the texture of your clothes, or press your hands against a firm surface.

    3 THINGS YOU CAN HEAR

    Listen for ambient background sounds: air conditioner, distant traffic, or your own breath.

    2 THINGS YOU CAN SMELL

    Notice the air, your clothing, or inhale deeply near coffee or soap.

    1 THING YOU CAN TASTE

    Take a slow sip of cold water or swallow gently, noticing the physical sensation.
